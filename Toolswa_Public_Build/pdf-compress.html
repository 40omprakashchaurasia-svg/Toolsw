Compress PDFs in your browser with Ghostscript WebAssembly. Your PDF is processed on your device and is not uploaded to a Toolswa server.

Important: Ghostscript generally preserves selectable/searchable text, but it may change or remove some PDF features such as forms, annotations, signatures, bookmarks, metadata or advanced colour profiles. Compression results vary by file. Digitally signed PDFs may no longer have a valid signature after modification. Keep your original file.

The first run downloads the compression engine. Large PDFs may take time and use significant memory.
